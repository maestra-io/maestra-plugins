Flow by flow

Welcome with an A/B test

#1001 · starts on the event "customer registered" · 3 problems + 1 suggestion
High
Problems
The A/B test is built on a random split instead of the A/B test block. A 50/50 split block: one branch SMS, the other email.
Rebuild it on the A/B test block — that one pins a customer to a single variant. A random split sends the same customer into different variants on re-entry, which distorts the comparison.
No subscription check for the channel before sending. The SMS branch has no subscription check at all; before email only the brand subscription is checked.
Check the subscription for the channel actually being sent (and for the email's topic, where it has one): SMS before the SMS step, email before the email step. A brand subscription is not consent for a channel.
No contact-validity check. There is no contact check before the send steps.
Add "valid email" before the email step and "valid phone" before the SMS step — without them undelivered messages rise and sender reputation takes the hit.
Suggestions (optional)
The segment filter sits after the start and after a day-long wait. A condition straight after the 1-day wait (customers outside the segment leave the flow).
If the filter is purely static, move it into the launch conditions so customers outside the audience never enter and never wait a day for nothing (saves load). Keep it as its own step if you need the drop-off visible in the funnel. A load-vs-clarity trade-off, not a defect.
Check manually
Whether the emails carry a subscription topic — that decides whether to check the topic or the channel.

Scheduled reactivation

#1004 · starts on a schedule
OK
✓ No serious problems found.
Suggestions (optional)
The checks run as separate steps after the start. Several filter conditions in a row after the schedule start block.
Some of these could move into the schedule's launch conditions, cutting the audience before entry and lowering load. If the steps are deliberately separate — to see where the audience drops out — that is fine, leave them. A load-vs-clarity trade-off.
Check manually
Whether the email carries a subscription topic — that decides the level of the subscription check (topic or channel).
